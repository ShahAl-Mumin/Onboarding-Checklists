# Onboarding Checklists

Free, practical onboarding checklists for UK HR teams, written from eight years of HR experience across the NHS, Civil Service, Higher Education and the non-profit sector.

## Checklists

| Checklist | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [New Starter: First Week](new-starter-first-week.md) | Making a new starter's journey from accepted offer to the end of week one calm and organised |
| [Right to Work Check (UK)](right-to-work-check.md) | Running a compliant right to work check, from choosing the route to recording the outcome |
| [Reference Request Process (UK)](reference-request-process.md) | Requesting, chasing, checking and recording references, with email templates |
| [Pre-Employment Checks: Overview (UK)](pre-employment-checks-overview.md) | Choosing which checks a role needs (DBS, occupational health, qualifications and more) and running them in a sensible order |

## How to use them

Copy a checklist into your own document or system, then change the owners, timings and wording to suit your organisation. Where a task has a suggested owner, it is shown in brackets.
